# Family Dashboard Feature - User Guide

## Overview

The Family Dashboard feature allows you to share your health journey with trusted family members and caregivers. This feature enables collaborative health monitoring, progress sharing, and emergency coordination while maintaining your privacy and control.

## Key Features

### 1. **Family Member Management**

Add and manage family members in your care circle:

- **Add Members**: Invite family members, caregivers, or trusted friends
- **Member Information**: Name, relationship, contact details
- **Roles**: 
  - **Primary**: Full access to health data and analytics
  - **Secondary**: Limited access to health data and alerts
  - **Viewer**: Read-only access to basic health information
- **Permissions**: Granular control over what each member can see
- **Notification Preferences**: Configure how each member receives updates

### 2. **Health Data Sharing**

Control what health information is shared with each family member:

- **Selective Metric Sharing**: Choose which metrics to share
  - Health Score
  - Steps & Activity
  - Heart Rate
  - Walking Steadiness
  - Sleep Hours
  - Fall Risk
- **Update Frequency**: 
  - Real-time: Updates as they happen
  - Daily Summary: Once per day
  - Weekly Summary: Once per week
  - On Demand: Only when you share
- **Location Sharing**: Option to include location data
- **Emergency Data**: Share emergency information with authorized members

### 3. **Progress Sharing**

Share health milestones and achievements:

- **Share Types**:
  - **Achievements**: Completed goals and milestones
  - **Improvements**: Health metric improvements
  - **Milestones**: Significant health events
  - **Updates**: General health updates
  - **Concerns**: Health concerns to share
- **Reactions**: Family members can react with emojis (❤️, 🎉, 👏, etc.)
- **Comments**: Optional comments on shared progress
- **Privacy Controls**: Choose who sees each share

### 4. **Activity Timeline**

Track all family-related activities:

- **Activity Types**:
  - Health updates
  - Alerts and notifications
  - Milestones achieved
  - Member additions/removals
  - Permission changes
- **Read/Unread Status**: Track which activities you've seen
- **Severity Indicators**: See importance of each activity
- **Chronological View**: Timeline of all activities

### 5. **Emergency Features**

Quick access to emergency functions:

- **Emergency Call**: One-tap emergency calling
- **Send Check-in**: Send status update to family
- **Share Location**: Share current location
- **Test Alert System**: Verify emergency notifications work
- **Health Status Display**: Current health summary for family

### 6. **Permissions Management**

Granular permission control:

- **View Health Data**: See health metrics and scores
- **View Location**: See current location
- **Receive Alerts**: Get emergency and health alerts
- **View Emergency Info**: Access emergency contact information
- **View Analytics**: See detailed analytics and trends
- **Manage Settings**: Change family dashboard settings

## How to Use

### Adding Family Members

1. Navigate to **Family Dashboard** → **Members** tab
2. Click **Add Member**
3. Fill in member information:
   - Name (required)
   - Relationship (required)
   - Email (optional)
   - Phone (optional)
4. Select role (Primary, Secondary, or Viewer)
5. Configure permissions
6. Set notification preferences
7. Click **Save**

### Sharing Health Data

1. Navigate to **Sharing** tab
2. Select a family member
3. Choose which metrics to share
4. Set update frequency
5. Enable location/emergency data if desired
6. Settings save automatically

### Sharing Progress

1. Navigate to **Progress** tab
2. Click **Share Progress**
3. Select share type
4. Enter title and description
5. Add value/unit if applicable
6. Choose who to share with
7. Click **Share**

### Viewing Activities

1. Navigate to **Activity** tab
2. View chronological timeline
3. Click activities to mark as read
4. Filter by type or severity

### Emergency Features

1. Navigate to **Emergency** tab
2. Use quick action buttons:
   - Emergency Call
   - Send Check-in
   - Share Location
   - Test Alerts

## Best Practices

1. **Start Small**: Add 1-2 trusted family members first
2. **Review Permissions**: Regularly review what each member can see
3. **Share Selectively**: Only share what you're comfortable with
4. **Update Regularly**: Keep family informed of significant changes
5. **Use Reactions**: Encourage family engagement with reactions
6. **Privacy First**: Remember you control all sharing settings

## Privacy & Security

- **You Control Everything**: You decide who sees what
- **Granular Permissions**: Fine-tune access for each member
- **Revocable Access**: Remove members or permissions anytime
- **Local Storage**: Data stored locally in your browser
- **No Automatic Sharing**: Nothing is shared without your explicit action

## Troubleshooting

### Member Not Receiving Updates

- Check member is active
- Verify permissions include relevant access
- Check notification preferences
- Ensure sharing frequency is set correctly

### Can't Add Member

- Verify required fields are filled
- Check email/phone format if provided
- Ensure member doesn't already exist

### Sharing Not Working

- Verify member has view-health permission
- Check selected metrics are enabled
- Ensure sharing frequency is not "on-demand only"

## Getting Help

For additional support:
- Review permission settings
- Check activity timeline for errors
- Verify member status (active/inactive)
- Contact support if issues persist
