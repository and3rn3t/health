# AI Insights Feature - User Guide

## Overview

The AI Insights feature provides personalized, intelligent analysis of your health data. Using advanced algorithms and pattern recognition, it generates actionable recommendations, identifies potential health concerns, celebrates achievements, and predicts future trends based on your health metrics.

## Key Features

### 1. **Intelligent Analysis**

The AI analyzes multiple health metrics simultaneously:
- **Activity Levels**: Steps, walking patterns, exercise frequency
- **Cardiovascular Health**: Heart rate, heart rate variability
- **Balance & Stability**: Walking steadiness, gait patterns
- **Sleep Quality**: Sleep duration, sleep patterns
- **Overall Health Score**: Comprehensive health assessment

### 2. **Insight Types**

#### Recommendations
- **Purpose**: Actionable suggestions to improve your health
- **Examples**: 
  - "Increase daily activity to 7,000-10,000 steps"
  - "Add balance exercises to improve stability"
  - "Establish a consistent sleep schedule"
- **Features**: 
  - Priority levels (high/medium/low)
  - Impact scores (1-10)
  - Timeframes for implementation
  - Related metrics

#### Warnings
- **Purpose**: Alert you to potential health concerns
- **Examples**:
  - "Sleep duration below recommended levels"
  - "Balance metrics declining"
  - "Health score needs attention"
- **Features**:
  - Severity indicators
  - Actionable recommendations
  - Related risk factors

#### Achievements
- **Purpose**: Celebrate your health successes
- **Examples**:
  - "Excellent activity progress"
  - "Healthy sleep patterns maintained"
  - "Outstanding health score"
- **Features**:
  - Positive reinforcement
  - Progress recognition
  - Motivation to continue

#### Predictions
- **Purpose**: Forecast future health trends
- **Examples**:
  - "Activity levels improving - projected to reach X steps"
  - "Fall risk can be reduced by 30-50% with interventions"
- **Features**:
  - Confidence scores
  - Trend-based forecasts
  - Actionable interventions

#### General Insights
- **Purpose**: Provide context and understanding
- **Examples**:
  - "Cardiovascular fitness assessment"
  - "Metric correlation analysis"
- **Features**:
  - Educational content
  - Health context
  - Metric relationships

### 3. **Dashboard Card**

Quick access to top insights:
- **Compact View**: Shows 2-3 most important insights
- **Priority Sorting**: High priority insights appear first
- **Quick Actions**: One-click access to full insights
- **Visual Indicators**: Color-coded by priority and type

### 4. **Full Insights View**

Comprehensive analysis interface:
- **Tabbed Organization**: 
  - All Insights
  - High Priority
  - Actionable Items
  - Achievements
- **Detailed Information**: 
  - Full insight descriptions
  - Confidence scores
  - Impact ratings
  - Timeframes
  - Related metrics
- **Custom Queries**: Ask specific health questions

### 5. **Custom Health Questions**

Ask personalized questions:
- **Examples**:
  - "Why is my walking steadiness decreasing?"
  - "What exercises would help my balance?"
  - "How can I improve my sleep quality?"
- **Features**:
  - Personalized responses
  - Data-driven answers
  - Actionable recommendations

## How to Use

### Viewing Insights

1. **Dashboard Card**:
   - Navigate to Analytics section
   - View top insights in the AI Insights card
   - Click "View All" to see complete insights

2. **Full Insights Tab**:
   - Go to Analytics → AI Insights tab
   - Browse all insights organized by tabs
   - Filter by priority, type, or actionability

### Understanding Insights

#### Priority Levels
- **High**: Requires immediate attention
- **Medium**: Important but not urgent
- **Low**: Informational or positive feedback

#### Confidence Scores
- **90-100%**: Very reliable insight
- **75-89%**: Reliable insight
- **50-74%**: Moderately reliable
- **Below 50%**: Less reliable, more data needed

#### Impact Scores
- **8-10**: High impact on health
- **5-7**: Moderate impact
- **1-4**: Lower impact

### Acting on Insights

1. **Review High Priority Insights First**
   - Address urgent health concerns
   - Follow actionable recommendations
   - Consult healthcare providers if needed

2. **Implement Recommendations**
   - Follow suggested timeframes
   - Track progress
   - Adjust as needed

3. **Celebrate Achievements**
   - Recognize positive progress
   - Maintain healthy habits
   - Set new goals

4. **Use Predictions for Planning**
   - Prepare for future trends
   - Implement preventive measures
   - Adjust interventions

### Asking Custom Questions

1. Navigate to AI Insights tab
2. Scroll to "Ask About Your Health" section
3. Type your question
4. Click "Get AI Answer"
5. Review personalized response

## Best Practices

1. **Regular Review**: Check insights weekly
2. **Prioritize Actions**: Focus on high-priority insights first
3. **Track Progress**: Monitor how insights change over time
4. **Ask Questions**: Use custom queries for specific concerns
5. **Consult Professionals**: Use insights to inform healthcare discussions
6. **Stay Consistent**: Implement recommendations consistently

- All analysis performed locally in your browser
- No health data sent to external AI services
- Insights generated from your data only

## Troubleshooting

### No Insights Generated
- Ensure health data has been imported
- Check that metrics have sufficient data
- Wait for analysis to complete
- Refresh insights if needed

### Low Confidence Scores
- More historical data needed
- High data variability
- Irregular patterns detected

### Insights Not Relevant
- Review health data accuracy
- Check data quality scores
- Ensure recent data updates
- Regenerate insights
