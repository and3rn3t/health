# Fall Risk Analysis Feature - User Guide

## Overview

The Fall Risk Analysis feature provides comprehensive AI-powered assessment, monitoring, and intervention management for fall prevention. This feature helps users understand their fall risk, track changes over time, and take proactive steps to reduce risk.

## Key Features

### 1. **Risk Assessment Dashboard**

The main dashboard provides a comprehensive view of your current fall risk status:

- **Overall Risk Score**: A score from 0-100 indicating your current fall risk level
- **Risk Level Classification**: Categorized as Minimal, Low, Moderate, High, Severe, or Critical
- **Multi-Dimensional Analysis**: Breakdown across 5 key dimensions:
  - Gait Risk
  - Balance Risk
  - Environmental Risk
  - Physiological Risk
  - Behavioral Risk

### 2. **Historical Trend Tracking**

Track your fall risk over time with the History tab:

- **Time Range Filters**: View trends for 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, 1 year, or all time
- **Visual Charts**: Interactive line charts showing risk score progression
- **Trend Analysis**: Automatic calculation of improving, declining, or stable trends
- **Category Breakdown**: See how each risk dimension changes over time

**How to Use:**
1. Navigate to the "History" tab in the Fall Risk dashboard
2. Select a time range using the filter buttons
3. Review the trend chart and statistics
4. Switch to "Breakdown" view to see individual category trends

### 3. **Risk Factor Details**

Get detailed information about each risk factor:

- **Expandable Details**: Click on any risk factor to see:
  - Detailed explanation
  - Category and trend information
  - Impact weight on overall risk
  - Whether the factor is modifiable
  - Related interventions

**How to Use:**
1. In the Overview tab, find the "Primary Risk Factors" section
2. Click on any risk factor card
3. Review the detailed information
4. Click "Start" on recommended interventions to begin addressing the risk

### 4. **Population Comparison**

Compare your risk with others in your age group:

- **Percentile Ranking**: See where you stand (e.g., 25th, 50th, 75th percentile)
- **Age Group Benchmarks**: Compare against average risk for your age
- **Visual Comparison**: Bar charts showing your position relative to benchmarks

**How to Use:**
1. Navigate to the "Comparison" tab
2. Review your percentile ranking
3. See how your risk compares to the average for your age group
4. Use this information to set realistic goals

### 5. **Real-Time Monitoring**

Monitor sensor data in real-time for immediate fall detection:

- **Sensor Visualization**: Live display of:
  - Accelerometer data (motion and impact)
  - Gyroscope data (rotation and orientation)
  - Heart rate and variability
  - Overall stability metrics

- **Risk Indicators**: Automatic alerts for:
  - High impact detected
  - Rapid rotation detected
  - Elevated heart rate

**How to Use:**
1. Navigate to the "Real-time" tab
2. Click "Start Real-time Monitoring" to begin
3. Monitor the sensor data visualizations
4. Review any detected events in the "Recent Detection Events" section

### 6. **Intervention Progress Tracking**

Track your progress with recommended interventions:

- **Overall Progress**: See your risk reduction from baseline to current to target
- **Individual Intervention Tracking**: For each active intervention:
  - Completion rate
  - Adherence rate
  - Effectiveness measurement
  - Days active
  - User ratings

- **Completed Interventions**: View your history of completed programs

**How to Use:**
1. Navigate to the "Progress" tab
2. Review overall risk reduction progress
3. Check individual intervention metrics
4. Switch to "Completed" view to see finished interventions

### 7. **Report Export**

Export comprehensive fall risk reports:

- **PDF Reports**: Print-ready reports with all assessment details
- **JSON Export**: Machine-readable data for integration with other tools
- **CSV Export**: Spreadsheet-compatible data for analysis

**How to Use:**
1. Click the "Export Report" button at the top of the dashboard
2. Select your preferred format (PDF, JSON, or CSV)
3. The file will download automatically
4. PDF reports can be printed or shared with healthcare providers

## Understanding Your Risk Score

### Risk Levels

- **Minimal (0-10)**: Very low risk, excellent mobility and balance
- **Low (11-20)**: Low risk, good overall health
- **Moderate (21-40)**: Moderate risk, some areas need attention
- **High (41-60)**: High risk, significant intervention recommended
- **Severe (61-80)**: Very high risk, immediate action needed
- **Critical (81-100)**: Critical risk, urgent medical consultation recommended

### Temporal Predictions

The system provides three time-horizon predictions:

- **Short-term (1-4 hours)**: Immediate situational risk
- **Medium-term (24-72 hours)**: Activity-based risk patterns
- **Long-term (7-30 days)**: Health trajectory and intervention effectiveness

## Best Practices

1. **Regular Assessments**: Check your fall risk regularly to track changes
2. **Review Interventions**: Focus on modifiable risk factors first
3. **Track Progress**: Use the History tab to see long-term improvements
4. **Share Reports**: Export and share reports with healthcare providers
5. **Follow Recommendations**: Implement suggested interventions for best results

## Troubleshooting

### No Historical Data Showing

- Historical data is collected automatically as you use the feature
- If you're new to the feature, data will appear after your first few assessments
- Check that you're viewing the correct time range

### Sensor Data Not Appearing

- Ensure real-time monitoring is enabled
- Check that your device has the necessary sensors
- Verify browser permissions for device access

### Export Not Working

- Ensure pop-ups are allowed in your browser
- For PDF export, use your browser's print dialog
- Check that you have sufficient storage space

## Privacy & Data

- All data is stored locally in your browser
- Historical data is kept for up to 100 assessments
- Exported reports contain only your assessment data
- No data is shared without your explicit action
